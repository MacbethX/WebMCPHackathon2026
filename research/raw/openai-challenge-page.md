# OpenAI WebMCP Challenge page (raw extract, openai.com/webmcp-challenge, captured 2026-08-27)

Theme: "Build something we haven't seen before: an app that becomes meaningfully better when people and their agents can use it together." "10 days for exploring what's possible with WebMCP."

Judging (FAQ verbatim): "Projects will be evaluated on usefulness, originality, execution, thoughtful use of WebMCP, and the quality of the human-agent experience."

Dates: reg/submissions open Aug 25 12pm PT; deadline Sep 3 1pm PT; winners ~Sep 23 (may change). Livestream Aug 25 3pm PT (x.com broadcast). Office hours Aug 31 11am PT (Discord channel 1517482456964534312 in the OpenAI server).

Prizes, top 10 each: $3,000 cash (OpenAI), 1 year ChatGPT Pro, Codex Micro keyboard, OpenAI swag, plus sponsor prizes (Shopify, Google Chrome, Netlify, Cloudflare, Vercel, Render).

Judges: Sarah Drasner (Distinguished Engineer, Chrome), Andrew Galloni (VP Research & Innovation, Cloudflare), Jude Gao (MTS Vercel, Next.js Core, author of vercel/shop PR 498), Ilya Grigorik (Distinguished Engineer, Shopify), Alex Nahas (creator of MCP-B, built Duckboard demo), Sean Roberts (VP Applied AI, Netlify), Justin Rushing (Browser Agent Lead, OpenAI).

FAQ facts: existing apps allowed (add WebMCP to an existing app); test in ChatGPT in-app browser (WebMCP out of the box) or Chrome via flag or origin trial; submission = description + live app + repo + demo video via Devpost.

First-party example apps linked (hosted on *.openai.chatgpt.site, opened via chatgpt.com/codex/deeplink): 3D Modeling (codex-modeling-studio), Collaborative Writing (Margin: agent comments under its own identity), Crossword Builder, Wandernote (itinerary + comments), Data Exploration (Duckboard, DuckDB-Wasm, by Alex Nahas, on workers.dev).
